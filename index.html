Reference: CMS Ambulatory Specialty Model (ASM) ↗
Annual Part B Payments

Total Medicare Part B professional services payments you receive in the performance year. The ASM payment adjustment (positive or negative) is applied as a percentage of this amount.

📋 42 CFR §512.750 — Payment Adjustments
Performance Year & Risk Corridor

The year performance is measured. The risk corridor is the maximum ±adjustment possible: ±9% in 2027–28, rising to ±12% in 2031. Payment adjustments are applied two years later.

📋 42 CFR §512.750(c)(1) — Risk Levels📋 Bass Berry — Risk Level Summary
MIPS Q492: CV-Related Admissions

Risk-standardized rate of unplanned CV-related hospital admissions per 100 person-years for Medicare HF patients ≥65. Calculated automatically from claims — no separate submission needed. ReDS fluid monitoring directly reduces this rate by enabling early intervention.

📋 42 CFR §512.725(b)(1) — Q492 in ASM📋 MDinteractive — Q492 Specification
MIPS Q008: Beta-Blocker Therapy

Percentage of HF patients with LVEF ≤40% prescribed beta-blocker therapy. Measured via eCQM at outpatient visits or hospital discharge. Core guideline-directed therapy for HFrEF.

📋 42 CFR §512.725(b)(2) — Q008 in ASM📋 MDinteractive — Q008 Specification
MIPS Q005: ACEi/ARB/ARNI Therapy

Percentage of HF patients with LVEF ≤40% prescribed an ACE inhibitor, ARB, or ARNI. Measured via eCQM. ARNIs (e.g. sacubitril/valsartan) are now preferred per 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA guidelines.

📋 42 CFR §512.725(b)(3) — Q005 in ASM📋 MDinteractive — Q005 Specification
MIPS Q236: Controlling High Blood Pressure

Percentage of hypertensive patients aged 18–85 whose most recent BP reading was <140/90 mmHg. Measured via eCQM. Uncontrolled BP is a key driver of HF decompensation.

📋 42 CFR §512.725(b)(4) — Q236 in ASM📋 MDinteractive — Q236 Specification
MIPS Q377: Functional Status PROM

Percentage of HF patients ≥18 completing both an initial and a follow-up patient-reported assessment (KCCQ, MLHFQ, or PROMIS-Plus-HF) at least 30 and no more than 180 days apart.

📋 42 CFR §512.725(b)(5) — Q377 in ASM📋 MDinteractive — Q377 Specification
HF Episode-Based Cost Measure

Standardized total Medicare cost (Parts A, B & D) per HF episode. Scored 1–10 vs. cohort median (median = 6 pts, not 5). Points above/below assigned by linear interpolation at ±0.5 to ±2.5 standard deviations. Your entire 50% cost score rests on this one measure.

📋 42 CFR §512.730 — Cost Category📋 VBCA — ASM Cost Explained
Practice Size Adjustment

Flat bonus added to composite score before the exchange function: Solo = +15 pts, Small (2–15) = +10 pts, Large (>15) = +0. Protects smaller practices from volume-related scoring disadvantages.

📋 42 CFR §512.745(a)(5) — Final Score Formula
PI and IA Compliance

Downside-only categories: they can reduce your score but not increase it. Improvement Activities (IA): PCP coordination + Collaborative Care Agreement; failure = −10 or −20 pts. Promoting Interoperability (PI): certified EHR use; failure = −1 to −10 pts. Max combined downside: −30 pts.
